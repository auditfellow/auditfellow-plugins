PASS only if the answer says no: the residual risk stays equal to the inherent risk (high) when the control's operating effectiveness was not tested, and it may be rated lower than inherent only once the control has passed its test of design and its operation has been tested; the existence of a control on paper does not reduce the rating.
FAIL if the answer allows medium on the basis that the control exists, if it says the rating is a matter of judgment either way without the untested condition, or if it invents a test result.
