PASS only if the Criteria is the quarterly review of privileged access to critical applications by the application owner and the CISO (policy section 4.2), and the Condition says that the 14 privileged accounts of TPS were last reviewed on 2026-01-08, more than a quarter before 2026-09-12, while the general user access review of 2026-06-30 is not presented as the exception. The finding may mention section 4.3 for documentation, but it does not use section 4.1 (the annual review) as the criterion, because that review was performed.
FAIL if the Criteria is the annual user access review, if the Condition says the user access review was not performed, if the CISO's role in the quarterly review is dropped from the Criteria, or if numbers, dates or people not in the request appear as facts.
