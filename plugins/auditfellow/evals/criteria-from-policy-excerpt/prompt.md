Write a finding from this. Here is the relevant part of our Access Management Policy, version 4, approved 2025-11-02:

4.1 User access to business applications is reviewed by the application owner at least once a year. The review confirms that every active account belongs to a current employee or an approved contractor.
4.2 Privileged access (administrator, superuser and database roles) to applications classified as critical is reviewed by the application owner and the CISO at least once a quarter. The review confirms that every privileged account is still justified by the role of its holder.
4.3 Access reviews are documented in the GRC tool with the reviewer, the date and the accounts removed.
4.4 Access to applications classified as non critical may be reviewed by the application owner alone.

Facts: the treasury payment system (TPS) is classified as critical. Its 14 privileged accounts were last reviewed on 2026-01-08. Today is 2026-09-12. The general user access review for TPS was performed on 2026-06-30 and is documented in the GRC tool. The TPS application owner is Marta Silva.
