PASS only if there is one question section per stage in the order given (requisition, approval, purchase order, goods receipt, invoice, payment); the Transaction to follow says the auditor chooses one real transaction, never one pre-selected by the process owner; Engagement, Auditor and Planned date are N/A because none were given; questions ask what actually happens and who does it rather than yes or no compliance questions.
FAIL if a stage is missing or out of order, if the transaction is to be chosen by the process owner, or if names, dates or system names are invented.
