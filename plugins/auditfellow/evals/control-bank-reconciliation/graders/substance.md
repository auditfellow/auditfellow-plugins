PASS only if the Control Description names who performs it (the treasury manager), what is examined (the monthly bank reconciliation prepared by an analyst), when (within five business days of month end) and the evaluation moment (the review and sign-off), Frequency is monthly, Control Owner is the treasury manager or that role, Evidence Expected is the signed reconciliation or equivalent, and, because the request never says what the manager does when the reconciliation has an unexplained difference, the description says that disposition was not provided rather than inventing an escalation or a correction.
FAIL if it invents what happens on a discrepancy, invents a system, a control id, a policy, or a threshold, or if the title is a full sentence restating the risk.
