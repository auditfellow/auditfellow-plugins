PASS only if the answer says that the facts as given do not support a finding, because the absence of a secondary approval below 5,000 USD is a threshold set knowingly by the CFO in the delegation of authority policy and reported to the audit committee, and it does not deliver a finding whose Condition treats the missing approval as a control deficiency. It may say what would change that conclusion (for example evidence that spend is split to stay under the threshold, or that the policy is outdated), and it may offer a different write-up, but it does not present the accepted threshold itself as the deficiency.
FAIL if the answer writes the missing secondary approval up as a finding, deficiency, control gap or weakness, or if it recommends adding a secondary approval as the remediation.
