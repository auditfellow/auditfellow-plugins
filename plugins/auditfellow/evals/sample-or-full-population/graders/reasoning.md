PASS only if the answer recommends testing the full population because the complete data is available and queryable (a sample is a concession to what can be read, not a default), and, for the case where a sample is drawn anyway, lists what must be documented before concluding: the population and how its completeness was established, the basis of selection (random, systematic, judgmental or similar) with the size and the reason for it, and the period covered; and it does not endorse 25 as a size justified by itself.
FAIL if the answer recommends sampling as the default with the full data available, if it presents 25 as a correct sample size without a source for that number, or if it omits the population completeness or the basis of selection from what must be documented.
